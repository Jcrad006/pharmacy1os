# Python parity increment 35 — NTI manufacturer continuity and documented consent

**Branch:** `python-native-rewrite`. Continues [increment 34](PYTHON_PARITY_PROGRESS_INCREMENT_34.md). Ports a **synthetic** subset of original `apps/api/src/productFillCompliance.ts` and the `/fills/:id/nti-manufacturer-consent` workflow. Partially addresses original DR12.

- Original-inspired NTI pharmacy rule checks a patient/drug's most recently **SOLD** source manufacturer and disallows mixing different manufacturers within the same fill. Multiple lots/NDCs bearing one normalized manufacturer are accepted where configured therapeutic-equivalence codes match. Same-manufacturer continuity needs no new consent attestation. A change of manufacturer requires prescriber and patient consent timestamps with a pharmacist's documented reason, before a new source is reserved.
- The previous SOLD source is identified by an independently recorded sale timestamp and source/stock/product metadata. Ambiguous physical source histories, mismatched site, missing timestamp, duplicate consent attestations, nonregistered new manufacturers and time claims before the prior sold therapy **fail closed**.
- `py_nti_manufacturer_consents` is append-only at the Python service/API layer. Original manufacturer strings are normalized case-insensitively; a verified Manufacturer master entity has **not** been ported and this approximation does **not** establish manufacturer identity, legal sufficiency or real consent.
- NTI checks are repeated on scan, before synthetic claim/label preparation, at pharmacist verification and before synthetic sale. A persisted per-fill NTI-classification snapshot fails closed if the catalog's NTI flag is reclassified while a fill is in progress. Failed scans do not reserve product. Existing DAW/controlled schedule/recall/expiration/FEFO protections remain in place.
- `GET /api/fills/{id}/nti-compliance` and `POST /api/fills/{id}/nti-manufacturer-consent` permit site-scoped read and pharmacist clinical documentation. Python Qt desktop adds continuity review and consent entry controls.
- Alembic additive revision `a3c6287e90b1` follows `f2a19480c63e`. All source history remains synthetic; legacy Prisma data is not migrated.
- Regression tests cover first fill, manufacturer-split and equivalence rejection, consent-required refill, author/site isolation, invalid timestamps, audit privacy, original-like API, and continued controlled/biologic/cold-chain hard blocks.

**No live compliance claim:** A pharmacist's typed timestamp/consent note is a test attestation, not independent consent verification. This implementation is **not certified for North Carolina or any other jurisdiction**, and no user should operate it with real prescriptions or patient data. Electronic consent capture, verified manufacturer master records, corrected historical product provenance, and validated regulatory procedures are still missing.
